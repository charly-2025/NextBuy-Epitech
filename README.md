# NextBuy: from raw data to smart decisions

> Built at **Epitech Rennes** · B-DAT-201 · Data · March 2026

## Overview

Exploration of a real-world dataset of millions of orders placed by thousands of online grocery customers (products, aisles, departments). The goal: turn raw data into meaningful indicators and actionable recommendations.

## Features

- Merging and typing several datasets (orders, products, aisles, departments)
- Best and worst selling products and aisles, most valuable customers
- Time analysis: busiest days and hours
- Most reordered products and reorder probability
- Per-customer profile: favorite product and favorite moment
- Prediction model training

## Tech stack

`Python` · `pandas` · `Jupyter` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn`
